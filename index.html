Our Story

Taste, the way it used to be.

Vuyala's Kittchen began with a simple belief — that real Telugu food needs no shortcuts. We bring together the bold curries of Andhra, the fiery, earthy cooking of Telangana, and the rustic Rayalaseema table under one roof.

We cook the old way. No artificial colours, no added flavours, no preservatives, no tasting salt — just honest spice, slow fire, and recipes handed down through generations.

Our home is built like a village hut — neutral, earthy, unhurried. No air-conditioning; we cool it the natural way. Food, and a feeling, you don't find anymore.
